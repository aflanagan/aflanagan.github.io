August Flanagan

Born and raised in rural Montana, where my family still keeps a homestead on the Missouri River. I studied biochemistry at the University of Washington and computer science at the University of British Columbia. A Bonderman Fellowship took me around the world for a year, followed by several years in Latin America — Cuba, Colombia, and Mexico — before I settled in the Bay Area.

I've founded two companies:

  • Babylist — founder and CTO; grew it from zero to $100M in annual revenue.
  • Cronitor — a bootstrapped SaaS I co-founded in 2014, still bootstrapped and making millions a year with no employees.

These days I think of myself less as a software engineer than a tastemaker, figuring out how to build across the world of AI agents. I'm tinkering on something new — more soon.

Each year I make a handful of angel investments and back a few nonprofits. Right now:

  • Common Ground Society
  • Montana Food Bank Network
  • Surfrider Foundation
  • Point Blue Conservation Science (Farallon Islands research station)

I split my time between Berkeley and Santa Cruz, surfing, skiing, and on the beach with my dog, Doc.
