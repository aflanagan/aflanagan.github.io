August Flanagan

Born and raised in rural Montana, where my family still keeps a homestead on the Missouri River. I studied biochemistry at the University of Washington and computer science at the University of British Columbia, and spent a year traveling the world as a Bonderman Fellow before landing in the Bay Area.

I've founded two companies:

  • Babylist
  • Cronitor

These days I think of myself less as a software engineer than as a tastemaker, working out how to build across the world of AI agents. I'm tinkering on something new — more on that soon.

Each year I make a handful of angel investments and back a few nonprofits. Right now that's the Common Ground Society, the Montana Food Bank Network, and Point Blue Conservation Science's Palomarin Field Station.

I live between Berkeley and Santa Cruz, and spend my free time surfing, skiing, and on the beach with my dog, Doc.
