August Flanagan

Born and raised in rural Montana, where my family still keeps a homestead on the Missouri River. I studied biochemistry at the University of Washington and computer science at the University of British Columbia, then spent a year traveling the world as a Bonderman Fellow and several years living in Latin America — Cuba, Colombia, and Mexico — before settling in the Bay Area.

I've founded two companies:

  • Babylist — as founder and CTO I grew it from zero to $100M in annual revenue. It's now the registry most expecting parents in America use to decide what they need for a new baby.
  • Cronitor — a bootstrapped SaaS I co-founded in 2014. Still bootstrapped, several million a year in revenue, zero employees.

These days I think of myself less as a software engineer than as a tastemaker, working out how to build across the world of AI agents. I'm tinkering on something new — more on that soon.

Each year I make a handful of angel investments and support a few nonprofits. Right now:

  • Common Ground Society
  • Montana Food Bank Network
  • Surfrider Foundation
  • Point Blue Conservation Science (Farallon Islands research station)

I live between Berkeley and Santa Cruz, and spend my free time surfing, skiing, and on the beach with my dog, Doc.
